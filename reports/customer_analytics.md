# Customer analytics

Generated by `python -m xsell analyze` from `sql/analysis/`. Rates show 95% Wilson intervals. All comparisons are descriptive and correlational, not causal.

Data: 13,647,309 customer-month rows, 956,645 customers, 17 monthly snapshots (all customers).
